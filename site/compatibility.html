The original WARBL (version 1) has no sensor stream and can't be used.

Firmware 4.8 sends WARBL's own fused elevation for tilt, where the experimental firmware sent raw accelerometer readings. If you use tilt, press Set zero now again after updating.

Known issues

  • A Mac that is also paired with WARBL over Bluetooth gets only about 10 frames/s over USB with firmware 4.8. The Mac sends the stream commands over both connections, and WARBL then streams over Bluetooth. A firmware fix has been proposed (amowry/WARBL2#46). Until then, disconnect WARBL's Bluetooth on the Mac while playing over USB.
  • Bluetooth keeps WARBL's loop at 3 ms while a Bluetooth connection is open, also when streaming over USB. It still plays well, with about 1 ms more delay.
