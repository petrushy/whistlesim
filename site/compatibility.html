Compatibility

WARBL firmware

WhistleSim supports only the official WARBL2 firmware, version 4.8 or later, from warbl.xyz and the WARBL2 releases. Until 4.8 is among the releases it is in beta, and its firmware file is in the WARBL2 repository.

WARBL2 firmwareWorks with WhistleSimUSBBluetooth
4.8 or later0.4.0 or laterAbout 500 frames/s (333 with a Bluetooth connection also open)Yes, in batches every 7.5–15 ms
4.7 or earlierNo: these have no sensor stream––

The original WARBL (version 1) has no sensor stream and can't be used.

Computers and phones

SystemStatus
macOS 12 or newer (Apple silicon and Intel)The main platform: developed and played on it.
Windows 10 or newer (x64)Builds and passes the same tests as macOS, but has had little playing yet. Reports welcome.
Linux x86_64 (glibc 2.39 or newer, e.g. Ubuntu 24.04)Builds and passes the same tests as macOS, but has had little playing yet. Reports welcome.
iPhone and iPadIn development. WARBL plugs straight into a USB-C iPhone, or pairs over Bluetooth.

Known issues

  • A Mac that is also paired with WARBL over Bluetooth gets only about 10 frames/s over USB with firmware 4.8 and WhistleSim 0.4.0, because 0.4.0 sends the stream commands over both connections and WARBL then streams over Bluetooth. Fixed in WhistleSim 0.5.0, which sends them only over USB when WARBL is on a cable. The firmware has been fixed too (amowry/WARBL2#46), in the version after 4.8. With WhistleSim 0.4.0 and firmware 4.8, disconnect WARBL's Bluetooth on the Mac while playing over USB.
  • Bluetooth keeps WARBL's loop at 3 ms while a Bluetooth connection is open, also when streaming over USB. It still plays well, with about 1 ms more delay.
