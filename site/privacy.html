Privacy policy

This policy covers the WhistleSim apps for macOS, Windows, Linux, iPhone and iPad.

WhistleSim collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network access, and nothing you do in it leaves your device.

What the app stores stays on your device: its settings, the instruments you save, the impulse responses you load and the recordings you make. On iPhone and iPad these are in the app's own storage, and the recordings and files are visible to you in the Files app. Deleting the app deletes them. On a computer they are in the folders listed in Getting started.

Bluetooth is used only to connect to a WARBL wind controller over Bluetooth MIDI, at your request.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages and sets no cookies. GitHub may log visits as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The download buttons ask GitHub for the newest release's files.
