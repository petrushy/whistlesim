Support

Questions, problems and ideas all go to WhistleSim's issue tracker on GitHub. You need a free GitHub account to write there.

Report a problem or ask a question Search existing issues

Before writing, check Getting started and the known issues.

What to include in a report

  • WhistleSim version: shown in the About window.
  • Computer and system: for example "MacBook Air M2, macOS 15.1" or "Windows 11, ASIO interface".
  • WARBL firmware version, and whether you play over USB or Bluetooth.
  • The Monitor tab's numbers while playing: frames/s, lost frames, the loop, and the share of frames with new hole readings. A screenshot is easiest.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened. For a sound problem, a short recording helps: Record saves the sound and every sensor reading, so the exact moment can be played back and examined.

Problems with WARBL itself

For WARBL's hardware, its MIDI playing or the Config Tool, see warbl.xyz. WhistleSim is a separate project; if you're not sure which one a problem belongs to, write here first.
